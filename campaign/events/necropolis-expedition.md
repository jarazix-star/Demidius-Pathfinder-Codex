# Necropolis Expedition

## Status

Verified campaign event recorded during the Culling. Exact calendar date,
mission objective, route, opposing forces, and sequence of individual deaths
remain unrecorded.

## Recorded returns and casualties

| Affiliation | Departed | Returned or remained | Named casualty outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Persephone's force | approximately 1,000 | approximately 400 survived | Kamryn died and was resurrected |
| Hades's force | approximately 1,000 | approximately 500 survived | Ezra died and refused resurrection |
| Hermes's force | approximately 250 | approximately 100 remained as ghouls | Leo died and could not be resurrected |
| Prince Luis's force | approximately 500 | approximately 100 survived | Prince Luis died and could not be resurrected |
| Party-aligned force | approximately 250 | approximately 200 survived | Pete died but Hermes restored him away from the field; Alley died and passed her God save |

The other named petty officers present survived. “Remained as ghouls” is
preserved exactly for Hermes's force; the record does not yet establish whether
those transformed personnel were counted as survivors, casualties, or a third
category by every faction.

## Consequences

The expedition was one of the Culling's major mass-casualty events and
demonstrated that resurrection outcomes were neither automatic nor uniform.
It also created lasting uncertainty about the status of transformed survivors
and about which divine powers could intervene beyond the battlefield.
