# Old Nysian Power Vacuum

## Confirmed situation

The destruction of Old Nysia's corrupt guild system also dismantled much of the
cities' infrastructure, administration, and legal order. Town guards are
quitting because garrisons have not been paid. Trash collection has stopped,
residents are dumping waste into rivers and the ocean, looting has spread, and
neighborhoods in Glistria are becoming guarded, increasingly self-sufficient
enclaves.

Roy, Demidius, and Siopi were the primary causes of the guild collapse. The
Culling did not create the underlying power vacuum, but intensified it through
panic, looting, and competition for supplies.

The Crown's men are attempting to restore order but are obstructed by Senate
resistance and bureaucratic red tape. Businesses are threatening to relocate to
Aevum or New Nysia unless the Crown offers concessions over its circumvention
of the Senate in pursuit of what critics describe as vigilante justice.

## Major factions

### The Champions

The Champions seized the Tradegulf garrison and are expanding through nearby
neighborhoods, roads, and the ferry route to Glistria. They are led by Oros of
the Blossom, a famous Battle Herald believed to be a quarter-godling of
Persephone. Oros survived fighting Hera during the Culling's opening battle,
his first Culling as an eligible participant.

Oros and an unidentified second survivor joined former guardsmen to take the
garrison. The Champions charge protection fees but patrol streets, suppress
crime, clean neighborhoods, and shelter refugees from the destroyed docks.
Many residents therefore consider their protection arrangement reasonable.

### The Crafter's Bow

The Crafter's Bow existed before the battle, opposes Old Nysian corruption, and
includes famous adventurers. It suppresses neighborhood violence while
organizing the looting and sacking of selected businesses in Tradegulf and
Glistria. Demidius is publicly rumored to be a founder, but that rumor is not a
confirmed fact.

### The Cobras

The Cobras grew from street thugs into a loose confederation of smaller gangs.
They hold some territory but primarily operate as criminals, thieves, and
violent opportunists across Tradegulf and Glistria.

## Imminent grand artifact auction

An auction offering some of the most valuable artifacts in the world is about
to occur at Arverdon Palace. The event is held only once every several years.

The auction artifacts were among the principal targets of Declan's raid.
Odysseus outmaneuvered the raiders by substituting false artifacts for the
collection they intended to steal. Declan's forces escaped with the fakes
rather than the genuine pieces. When and how Odysseus arranged the
substitution, the present custody of the real artifacts, and whether the
raiders have discovered the deception remain unrecorded.

The auction's host, rules, bidders, security, lots, and prices remain
unrecorded.
