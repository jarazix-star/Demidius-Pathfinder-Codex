# Recovered Artifacts and Magic Items

This record promotes the nine artifact and magic-item groups approved from the
July 2026 Discord export review. It preserves confirmed mechanics, ownership
at the time of the cited event, and explicit uncertainties without inventing
missing properties.

## 1. Eyebrow Piercing of Confidence

- +4 luck bonus to Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma.
- Fatal Flaw DCs increase by 5.
- Twice per day, the wearer may spend one Mythic Power as a swift action. The
  next spell cast within one round gains +5 to its save DC.

## 2. Pete's ioun-stone resonance service

- Normal ioun stone: 75% chance to resonate.
- Cracked ioun stone: 25%.
- Flawed ioun stone: 10%.
- A result of 100 can produce up to three resonant effects.
- The creator normally chooses set or random resonance; Pete's stock uses
  random resonance.

## 3. Gorgon artifact set

### Crown of the First Gorgon

- +10 untyped bonus on Intimidate checks.
- +5 to the DC of fear effects.
- Three times per day as a swift action, a 120-foot cone gaze petrifies targets
  for 1d6 rounds. The Fortitude DC is 20 + half the user's character level; a
  successful save instead staggers the target for one round.
- -10 penalty on Diplomacy checks.
- A weekly Will save resists progressive emotional muting.

### Fang of the Gorgon

- Immunity to petrification and poison from effects of Titan strength or less.
- Resistance to transformation and +2 on saves against Transmutation.
- Protection against aging.
- Beneficial and self-directed transformations still require a save.

### Serpent Lash of Stheno

- Can function as one powerful +5 whip or five lesser serpent lashes.
- Both modes carry poison and petrification risks.
- On a natural 1, the weapon can turn against its owner.

### Set interaction

- The Fang grants +2 untyped natural armor per other set piece, maximum +6.
- Using more than one piece risks transforming the user into a gorgon.
- Sacrificing the complete set was valued at three boons for a player character
  or two boons for a cohort.
- The Unblinking Idol Mask is associated with the set, but its complete
  mechanics were not preserved and remain unconfirmed.

## 4. Obsidian Crown

- +20 competence bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks.
- Adds a further +1 per caster level to Intimidate.
- Beating an Intimidate DC by 20 produces fear rather than merely shaken;
  beating it by 40 produces panic.
- Constant *true seeing* and *tongues*.
- The wearer can be healed by positive or negative energy.
- A curse associated with Hermes and Dionysus makes the wearer resemble a
  seven-foot skeleton and fuses the crown to the skull.

## 5. Healing demiplane

Anyone who sleeps in this demiplane becomes fully healed. On the first such
rest, the visitor also permanently gains 50 maximum hit points. The price is a
Loyalty Fatal Flaw toward the demiplane's owner.

## 6. Saena's Mirror

Saena's Mirror is a +3 mithral, light-fortification heavy steel shield.

- Missile Shield functions with the shield.
- Shield Focus can extend its defense to a ballista attack.
- Greater Spell Focus enables interaction with Ray Shield.
- Mythic Shield Focus can reflect magical ballista attacks and one area effect
  per hour.

The exact timing language was not fully preserved and must be checked against
the original table ruling before strict mechanical implementation.

## 8. Artifacts from later sea raiding

- **Echo Blade:** +5 sword whose more than thirty soul-and-memory echoes ignore
  voluntary attack penalties such as Power Attack but impose -10 Perception
  when their arguments and scorn overwhelm the wielder.
- **Tiara of the Ancients:** combines wisdom, knowledge, and the power of love.
  Its magical-girl transformation grants immunity to mind-affecting effects
  and additional powers requiring a catchphrase. Maarin claimed it.
- **Ring of Embers:** adds +3 damage per die of fire damage. Elemental spell
  damage becomes fire-only; force, sonic, and non-elemental magic remain
  available.
- **Halfhead's Halfblade:** exceptionally powerful dogslicer feared by canines,
  though not by horses.

## 9. Other preserved item leads

- **Fleet-Footed Sandals:** quickened *haste* once per day at caster level 15.
- **Rhopalon:** greatclub containing lion teeth and skull fragments; retained
  for auction.
- **Mirror of True Divination:** named in the treasure log, but its full
  properties were not supplied.
