## Recovered campaign catalog

The [Recovered Artifacts and Magic Items](../campaign/recovered-artifacts-and-magic-items.md)
record preserves nine newly approved Discord-source groups. It includes the
Gorgon artifact set, Obsidian Crown, healing demiplane, Saena's Mirror, Pete's
ioun-stone resonance rules, Silent Court distributions, later sea-raiding
finds, and incomplete item leads. Items without complete mechanics remain
research stubs rather than fully identified or mechanically available items.

## Research standard

Use the [Artifact Research Protocol](ARTIFACT_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md) before treating a newly recovered artifact as fully understood. Field observations, researched history, confirmed mechanics, and unresolved risks should remain separate in every artifact entry.
