# The Shard of First Light

![The Shard of First Light](../../assets/artifacts/auction/shard-of-first-light.png)

Sold for **750,000 gp**. This primordial-creation artifact predates time and
grants vision into unformed realms. Legends say it cracked at the dawn of
existence and its fragments scattered across the universe. Exact mechanics,
fragment count, buyer, and current owner remain unknown.
