# Ring of the Suzerain

![Ring of the Suzerain](../assets/artifacts/ring-of-the-suzerain.png)

The Ring of the Suzerain consolidates Demidius's Ring of Evasion, Ring of
Freedom of Movement, and Ring of the Ecclesiarch.

## Confirmed powers

- Evasion.
- Continuous *freedom of movement*.
- +5 competence bonus on Diplomacy checks.
- +5 competence bonus on Knowledge (religion) checks.
- Divine signet, seal, and marking functions.
- Twice the normal number of Leadership followers.
- Followers gain +4 morale on Will saves against enchantment.
- *Prayer* once per day.
- *Enter image* once per day.

Its price and destruction method are unknown. Functions that Hero Lab Classic
cannot automate remain part of the tabletop record.
