# Demidius Optimization Codex

![Demidius Thorne, his divine-growth details, and Hermes' Earrings of Arcane Refrain](assets/engines/demidius-optimization-codex.png)

This is the dedicated optimization hub for **Demidius Thorne**. It keeps build
theory, advancement planning, spell priorities, item interactions, and the
Five Pillars together without turning the campaign home page into a character
build document.

## The Five Pillars

Every proposed option should strengthen at least one of the Five Pillars.

1. **Probability:** control the die and insure campaign-defining rolls.
2. **Magical Supremacy:** decide whose magic remains active.
3. **Influence:** turn Charisma, trust, followers, and alliances into reach.
4. **Infrastructure:** control travel, recovery, logistics, and the battlefield.
5. **Divinity:** pursue mythic and divine growth without losing the moral center.

See [The Five Pillars of Demidius](../codex/01_PILLARS_OF_DEMIDIUS.md).

## Optimization engines

| Engine | Function |
|---|---|
| Probability | Luck bonuses, rerolls, post-roll intervention, and resource timing |
| Magical Supremacy | Dispels, counterspells, resistance penetration, and layered control |
| Influence | Charisma, Leadership, diplomacy, command, and institutions |
| Infrastructure | Dawnrunner operations, demiplane access, weather, travel, and logistics |
| Divine Progression | Godly powers, mythic advancement, boons, templates, and Fatal Flaws |
| Action Economy | Swift, immediate, free, and standard-action competition |
| Charisma | Dependencies flowing from Demidius's primary ability score |
| Risk Management | Failure modes, dangerous artifacts, domination, and contingency planning |

## Immediate priorities

1. Acquire *wandering weather* as soon as possible for mobile fleet-scale
   weather control.
2. Preserve Mythic *greater dispel magic* as a signature encounter tool.
3. Continue evaluating every bonus through the campaign's luck-conversion
   rulings and action-economy limits.
4. Treat the Dawnrunner, officers, followers, and strategic bases as part of
   the character build.
5. Keep campaign facts, rules-as-written, GM rulings, and recommendations
   explicitly separated.
