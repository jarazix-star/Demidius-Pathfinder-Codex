# The Isles of Berres

The **Isles of Berres** are Zatera's great western archipelago and the principal maritime region of *The Arrival*. Poseidon's curse over the Shipbreaker Sea disrupts ordinary instruments and hides the moon and stars at night, making the Pirate Lords' fourteen Wayfinders central to travel and political power.

## Mapped Berresian regions

- **Sunlit Chain:** Declan's former domain and the best-documented regional chart.
- **Sounon:** the campaign's starting island and the intended shared home of the Dawnrunner and Matcha Frappuccino crews.
- **Stormspire:** the Storm King's floating city, marked within the Sunlit Chain.
- **Misthold:** Rosalind Galeheart's cloud-covered territory. Its detailed chart is canonical, but its exact position relative to the Sunlit Chain remains unknown.

## Mapped destinations beyond Berres

- **Old Nysia:** a separate Nysian region containing Tradegulf, Arverdon Palace, and Glistria.
- **Glistria:** a detailed city map within Old Nysia.
- **Zatera:** the continental-scale world map establishing the Isles' relationship to Nysia, Lodingen, Prathia, and the other great lands.

The reader-facing version is published as the **Isles of Berres** wiki page and serves as the geographic drill-down from **Zatera**.
