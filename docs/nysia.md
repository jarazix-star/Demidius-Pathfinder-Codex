# Nysia

![Zatera world map showing Nysia in the far southwest](assets/locations/zatera-150-pa-world-map.jpg)

Nysia is the southwestern continent of Zatera. This spelling follows the
supplied world map and is canonical throughout the project.

## Geographic hierarchy

1. **Zatera** — the campaign world.
2. **Nysia** — the southwestern continent.
3. **Old Nysia** — a historical region within the continent.
4. **Glistria** — a major fortified coastal city within Old Nysia.

The historical regional map identifies Tradegulf, Glistria, Arverdon Palace,
the Acis River corridor, and numerous surrounding settlements. Old Nysia is
not a synonym for the entire continent.

## Old Nysia

![Map of Old Nysia](assets/locations/old-nysia-map.jpg)

Old Nysia contains the best documented Nysian campaign locations. The region
is the setting for the Battle for Tradegulf, the collapse of the old guild and
legal systems, and the resulting power vacuum and relief efforts.

## Glistria

See [Glistria](glistria.md) for the detailed city map, districts, landmarks,

## Current government

Nysia is a semi-constitutional monarchy ruled by Queen Lidda Beaumont from
Arverdon Palace. The exact modern borders, capital status, provincial
divisions, and relationship between Old Nysia and New Nysia remain
incompletely recorded.
