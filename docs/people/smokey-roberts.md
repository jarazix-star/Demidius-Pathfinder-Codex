# Smokey Roberts

![Interpretive portrait of Smokey Roberts](../assets/people/smokey-roberts.png)

Smokey Roberts is a pirate lord, demigod of Athena, father of Demidius, Siopi,
Paradox, and many other children, former employer of Tenor, and patron of the
agent Nyssa. His precise appearance, class, level, and territory are not yet
confirmed.
