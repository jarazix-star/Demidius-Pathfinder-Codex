# Tenor

![Tenor performing the bloodline rite](../assets/events/tenors-bloodline-rite.png)

Tenor was a level 15 gunslinger, Smokey Roberts's **Left Hand**, and the
now-deceased fixer of Smokey's organization. He was unusually weak for his
rank because he willingly surrendered his potential and much of his power in
two rituals.

The first ritual prevented Tenor from ever becoming stronger in exchange for
ending his aging. The second cost him ten levels—reducing him from level 25 to
level 15—and one eye. In return, he could perceive and understand all magic,
including divine magic and unrealized divine potential.

Tenor used this sight to recruit demigods, quarter godlings, and many of
Smokey's children before their abilities manifested. He treated those children
as valuable tools and raised powerful soldiers loyal to himself and Smokey.
Although exploitative, this was less openly murderous than the attitude of
other lieutenants who wanted Smokey's children killed.

Tenor also created the bloodline rite that allowed one of Smokey's children to
absorb part of a sibling's power by killing that sibling. The rite later
produced a mortal god during the Misthold tournament.

Tenor's cause of death and the precise limits of his magical sight remain
unknown.
