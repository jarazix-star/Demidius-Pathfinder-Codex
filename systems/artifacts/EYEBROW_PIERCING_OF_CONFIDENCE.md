# Eyebrow Piercing of Confidence

Demidius's legendary item grants +4 luck to Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma,
increased to +6 by Fortune's Child and Fate's Favored. It also increases the DC
of Fatal Flaws by 5.

Twice per day, the wearer may spend one Mythic Power as a swift action. The
next spell the wearer casts within one round gains +5 to its save DC. Hero Lab
automates the mental ability-score bonuses; the Fatal Flaw modifier and this
twice-daily Mythic Power ability are tracked manually because Hero Lab Classic
has no native campaign subsystem for either effect.

The ability-score increase is not itself +6 directly to every derived roll; recalculate the corresponding ability modifiers from the adjusted scores.
