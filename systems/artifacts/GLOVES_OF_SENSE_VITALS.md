# Gloves of Sense Vitals

While equipped, the gloves grant **+5d6 sneak attack damage**.

Hero Lab Classic correctly adds the five sneak-attack dice. Its restriction on
changing equipment during a polymorph effect is an interface limitation, not
an additional artifact rule.
