# Gorgon Artifact Set

This dangerous set is associated with the immortal gorgon sisters. Using more
than one piece risks transforming the bearer into a gorgon. The complete set
may be sacrificed for three boons for a player character or two for a cohort.

## Crown of the First Gorgon

- +10 untyped bonus on Intimidate checks.
- +5 to the save DC of fear effects.
- Three times per day as a swift action, project a 120-foot cone gaze.
  Targets that fail a Fortitude save (DC 20 + half the user's character level)
  are petrified for 1d6 rounds; a successful save leaves the target staggered
  for one round.
- -10 on Diplomacy checks.
- A weekly Will save resists the crown's emotional muting.

## Fang of the Gorgon

- Immunity to petrification and poison from effects of Titan strength or less.
- Resistance to transformation and aging.
- +2 on saves against Transmutation effects.
- Beneficial and self-directed transformations still require a saving throw.
- Grants +2 untyped natural armor per other set piece worn, to a maximum of +6.

## Serpent Lash of Stheno

The lash may function as one +5 whip or split into five lesser serpent lashes.
Both forms carry poison and petrification risks. On a natural 1, a lash may
turn against its owner.

## Unblinking Idol Mask

The mask is confirmed as part of the set, but its individual mechanics have
not yet been recovered.
