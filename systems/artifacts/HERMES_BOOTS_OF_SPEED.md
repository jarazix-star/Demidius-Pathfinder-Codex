# Hermes's Boots of Speed

Gifted by Hermes to Demidius.

## Continuous

- Functions as **boots of striding and springing** (+10 enhancement land speed; +5 competence on Acrobatics checks made to jump).
- Functions as **winged boots** (fly 60 ft., average; 3/day for up to 5 minutes per flight).

## Daily powers

- **3/day:** as a free action, cast **quickened *haste*** at **CL 15**.
- **1/day:** add **+5** to one spell's saving-throw DC.

The free-action *haste* preserves the swift action for other tools. Flight uses and *haste* uses are separate daily pools. The +5 DC should be reserved for an encounter-defining spell after relevant immunities and protections are removed.
