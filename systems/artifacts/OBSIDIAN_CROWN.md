# Obsidian Crown

- +20 competence bonus on Diplomacy and Intimidate checks.
- An additional +1 per caster level on Intimidate checks.
- Exceeding an Intimidate DC by 20 causes fear rather than merely shaken;
  exceeding it by 40 causes panic.
- Constant *true seeing* and *tongues*.
- The wearer may be healed by positive or negative energy.

The crown bears a curse associated with Hermes and Dionysus: it fuses to the
wearer's skull and makes the wearer appear as a seven-foot skeleton.
