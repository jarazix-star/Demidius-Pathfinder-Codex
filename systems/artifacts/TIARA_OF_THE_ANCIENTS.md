# Tiara of the Ancients

The tiara is associated with wisdom, knowledge, and love. Speaking its
catchphrase produces a magical-girl transformation that grants immunity to
mind-affecting effects and additional powers that have not yet been revealed.

Maarin claimed the tiara. Its complete mechanics and catchphrase remain
unrecorded.
